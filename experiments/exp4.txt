EXPERIMENT 4
Configure DHCP Server and Wireless Router and Check Connectivity

AIM:
To configure a DHCP server and wireless router, connect wireless devices and verify DHCP addressing and connectivity.

STEPS:

1. Configure the DHCP server.
2. Configure FastEthernet on the server.
3. Configure the wireless router.
4. On the laptop, remove the wired NIC card.
5. Select/add the wireless card.
6. Connect the laptop to the wireless network.
7. Connect the laptop with the wireless router.
8. Enter the wireless security key.
9. On the PC, remove the wired NIC card.
10. Select/add the wireless card.
11. Repeat the wireless connection steps.
12. Check the DHCP IP address assigned to the PC.
13. Check the DHCP IP address assigned to the laptop.
14. Verify that both devices received addresses from the DHCP server.
15. Check the wireless connection status.
16. Test connectivity between the connected devices.
17. Confirm that the devices can communicate through the wireless router.

RESULT:
The DHCP server and wireless router were configured. The PC and laptop received DHCP IP addresses and network connectivity was checked.
